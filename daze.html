Daze
Daze
The Prophet
A very observant lead singer whose songs brought up clever observations about the world and the current political turmoil, Daniel Zane Laurent, was turned into a vampire one fateful day, on a whim by an older Malkavian who sought to leave offspring and took into consideration the clever and witful insight of the young boy. Attacked on a highway, the van which he drove along with his bandmates was driven off the road and soon the attack came, turning him instantly.
It was a clever rouse by it's sire, as Daze had been turned right outside city limits, no Camarilla law had been broken. Now plagued with dreams of the future, he has come to understand the inner machinations of the city's inner council and has pledged to help Arron into becoming the next Prince of the city. What he thought was a curse to begin with, he's come to see as a blessing... If only the voices in his head weren't so loud. ❧

Plot Hooks.

Prophecy. He is good and attuned to hear the voices around him and uses those to create pieces of music that at times, it may bring an idea of what the future entails.

Signature Motif.

Hyperactivity. Daniel was always a problem kid at school because he could not focus and had issues performing. The reality was that at that age he was an undiagnosed TDAH kid who grew up resented and depressed. In this unlife, however, he has learned to embrace his desire to find pleasure and his hyperfocus.

Perfect for...

Raunchy and Romance threads Because he is neurodivergent and there is a dopamine drive, he will probably have the biggest love bombing scenes with your muse and give you the biggest sex experience... only to dissappear an hour later into some other unknown but interesting venture.
Goal

Tranquility.

The world is becoming incresingly dangerous to the Camarilla vampires. He knows it well. Thus why he is been helping his Coterie of vampires to achieve a certain level of Tranquility in the city to avoid prying eyes from their enemies...
obstacle

Vampiric Nature.

...however, no matter how he tries to fight it, it's impossible to make a dent or a change. Vampires are meant to be forces of decay and corruption and their sole existence is what makes ails this world. He knows it, but doesn't want to accept it. And perhaps, he never will.
