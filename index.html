About the blog

A multi-muse, multi-ship, single-verse roleplaying blog unfurls within the shadowed streets of Montréal by Night, where a diverse gathering of Night Folk dances through the city's secretive waltz, each playing their enigmatic part in the unseen ballet. At the heart of this tale is The Sagittarius Society, a coalition of supernatural beings bound by a shared purpose. Each member, from their own mysterious corner of existence, guides their kin toward a harmonious path, striving for a world where all may dwell in Concordia. And yet, their delicate balance is constantly at risk.

The blog chronicles the strange and mystical journeys of these individuals, united as allies, as they navigate the treacherous waters of power and intrigue. Yet, the path to greatness is fraught with opposition: ravenous vampires, fierce werewolves, capricious faeries, judgmental demons, merciless mages, and vengeful wraiths all lurk in the shadows, each hungering for the power and influence that binds the mortals of the city, which the Society has claimed as their home. These are the eerie tales of a fragile coalition, barely holding together, as they safeguard the very fabric of reality within the city’s dark heart.

  • «Urban Fantasy»

    Threads are set inside the Urban Fantasy genre, specifically within the World of Darkness. The blog's story is set up around Montréal, QC.

  • «Open Shipping»

    Characters are polyamorous and not interested in monogamy. What they do with your muse will affect other threads and they may act on consequence.

  • «Open To Crossover»

    Characters are pretty oblivious to other species and interested in learning from other magical individuals. They won’t know anything about crossover lore.
This blog takes inspiration from different lines of the table top roleplaying games that conform the World of Darkness. Prior knowledge of the game or universe is not required to interact with this blog or the characters.

Limits & Expectations

My intention is to not only have an amendable thread, but to have a human and friendly relationship with other writers such as me. For this very purpose, these are the limits (set for me, on what I will do in this blog) and the expectations (set on you and what I am expecting from you.) If you have read them, please send me a DM with a pink heart emoji and I will use said message to start us plotting.
Limits
  • Communication first.Feel free to come at me with any desires or issues you may have with my writing. I'd be more than happy to listen to you!
  • Limited Gif use. I don't always use gifs or icons for my replies. You can use whatever you want, but the image sometimes is limited.
  • Use of AI tools. I use mainly Midjourney to create most of the images in the blog and when I need a reaction image. If this is something that bothers you, please don't interact with me.
  • M/M erotic writing only. If you are interested in writing gay smut, I am available. Any other relationships with females are available except romantic and sexual.
  • Open to Cross Overs. I am happy to adapt and mesh the verse from where your character is coming to my own. Let me know in advance if you'd like something like this.
  • All my Faceclaims are Sagittarius. Yes, you heard that right. While some muses remain with FCs who are not Sagittarius, most if not all were blessed by the Archer. I can create new muses for a thread, so long they are of said sign.
Expectations
  • Patience required. I'm a very slow writer and I will mostly run the replies on queue. If you wish me to give priority to a thread of ours, let me know.
  • Reply speed. I do not expect any speed, but a good reply every two weeks works for me. If for some reason you can't continue, let me know. If I see that you constantly drop my threads I will no longer interact with you.
  • Adults only. Minors do not interact. If you are a minor in your country, do not interact with me. It is your responsibility to let me know your age before we start plotting.
  • No Godmodding / Metagame.
  • Cut posts. Please cut your posts. If you don't know how to, let me know and I'll be glad to show you or do it for us.
  • One paragraph per reply minimum. I expect at least a good decent paragraph of at least 5 to 10 lines per reply in order for me to play with you.
  • No relationship with Cobalt/Dave and their blogs. In the past this player had stolen content created by me (more of that here) and while I have no receipts to show other than my accusation (because, as of September 17th, 2023, they have modified their blogs to not reflect the association between us) I still remain firm on my limit. As a personal rule, I will not be interacting in character with anyone who interacts with them.
