Ashton Baudelaire
Ashton Baudelaire
The Apprentice
Ashvindra, known in the mundane world as Ashton Baudelaire, is a dreamer with calloused fingers and stars in his eyes. Once a street performer with nothing but a worn-out guitar and a voice full of yearning, Ashton’s fae soul was reborn when the Dreaming stirred within him. Guided by Ace Diamandis, he entered the path of squirehood under the luminous banner, learning not only swordplay but also the sacred duty of beauty and inspiration. With Dio Thunderbolt, his closest companion, he co-founded Stormrider, a rock band known in both realms for songs that echo like oaths and shimmer with Glamour. Ashvindra lives at the edge of two worlds — the gutter and the heavens — playing chords that remember both.
Though young and unproven, Ashvindra burns with the silent fire of the truly destined. The courts may see him as a mere squire, a scruffy musician barely worthy of courtly grace, but deep within him lies the soul of a warrior-poet. Every note he plays is a step away from Banality, every duel he trains for a rebellion against fate’s dismissal. He watches Ace not only as a mentor, but as a symbol of what he might become — adorned in honor, beloved, and bathed in glory. Whether beneath faerie moonlight or city neon, Ashvindra carries his guitar like a knight’s sword, playing until the stars themselves listen.❧

Plot Hooks.

Rebellion. To him, life is to be lived fast and quick. Every moment is an opportunity to grow and to live and he doesn't want to miss a thing of having the full experience, the more dangerous and problematic, the better. With a lover in every port, this Satyr is ready to enjoy life experiences.

Signature Motif.

Moving. He is a very active individual who is always moving, jumping and nevery really quite sitting. He likes to feel his body moving and acting.

Perfect for...

Romantic love If you are looking for a romantic guy who will make your guy feel good, try him. He is a great boyfriend but don't expect him to be domestic. Cheating threads and dangerous couples will always be preferred by him.
Goal

Inspiration.

Ashvindra believes in the power of music to awaken the Dreaming. He seeks to compose a legendary ballad that transcends mortal ears, a song so potent in emotion and truth that it rekindles Glamour in even the most Banality-scarred hearts. He wants it to be remembered long after his name fades—a melody that binds changelings together in hope.
obstacle

Expectations.

True inspiration eludes him, especially as pressure mounts and self-doubt creeps in. He’s haunted by the fear that his talent is ordinary, that the world doesn’t need another song, and that his voice may not be strong enough to battle the numbness of reality. Add to that Dio's rising fame and Ace’s expectations, and Ashvindra finds himself drowning in the very silence he’s meant to shatter.
«Whatever tomorrow brings, I'll be there.»
