Ace Diamandis
Ace Diamandis
The Crooner
Acetides, once the sparkling heartthrob of the fae-infused boy band Royal Straight Flush, now walks the human world as Ace Diamandis, a charismatic vocal coach for rising stars. Each member of the band embodied a card suit, and as the Ace, Acetides was both the beginning and the wild card, embodying spontaneity, charm, and hidden power. Beneath his stylish exterior and radiant smile lies a Changeling's soul forged in the fires of performance, glamour, and the bittersweet memory of lost Arcadia. Though the limelight has dimmed, his voice carries the echo of dreams, a reminder of the glory days when dreams themselves danced to his tune.
By night, Ace sheds the glitter of mortal fame to don the mantle of knight in the Seelie Court, a sworn protector of beauty, harmony, and wonder in the Dreaming. His dual life is a delicate balancing act between mortal mentorship and fae duty, yet he thrives within it, ever the performer, ever the guardian. Acetides serves not only with blade and voice, but with passion—a guiding star to the younger Changelings who still tremble at the threshold of disbelief and enchantment. In a world that often forgets to dream, Acetides remembers, and he sings it back into being.❧

Plot Hooks.

Music and Teaching. He still remembers his old days and wishes to go back, but with all the scandals, he can barely keep it as a piano and singing teacher. He is looking a royal to offer his loyalty to, one that he can both protect and lead.

Signature Motif.

Dancing. He is a very active individual who is always moving, jumping and nevery really quite sitting. He likes to feel his body moving and acting.

Perfect for...

Romantic love If you are looking for a romantic guy who will make your guy feel good, try him. He is a great boyfriend and even better if you want it with a side of furry.
Goal

Inspiration.

Acetides dreams of rekindling the light of Glamour in the hearts of young Changelings through the transcendent power of music. As a vocal coach by day and knight by night, he believes art is more than entertainment—it’s a sacred thread to the Dreaming. He teaches not just melody, but emotion, presence, and the ability to channel one's fae soul. For him, each voice awakened is a spark of hope in a dimming world.
obstacle

Disbelief.

However, the modern world is growing colder, harder, and far less inclined to believe. His students, though talented, carry the weight of cynicism—raised in a culture that mocks wonder and worships logic. Many are numb to beauty, and those who do glimpse the Dreaming often fear it. Banality clings to them like smog. Acetides struggles daily to pierce that fog, realizing that talent alone isn’t enough—he must ignite belief in a world that’s forgotten how.
