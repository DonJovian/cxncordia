Alder Beauregard
The Aristocrat
Alder Beauregard, known in the Dreaming as Albrecthine of House Fiona, is a Sidhe noble whose soul recently burst forth from the shimmering gates of Arcadia. A living fragment of legend wrapped in mystery and moonlight, Albrecthine walks among mortals with the grace of centuries. His mortal host, Alder, was a doctor, a dreamer, and a soul sensitive enough to bear the weight of a fae spirit. Now reborn, Albrecthine has embraced the city’s chaos as a new tapestry to shape with purpose and beauty.
Radiant yet fierce, Albrecthine has taken Acetides and Ashvindra under his protection, naming them his knights—his blades in the mortal world. As a scion of House Fiona, he burns with passion, longing to inspire mortals and changelings alike with the majesty of the old courts. Though he speaks with elegance and reverence, his gaze is always turned toward destiny, for he believes the Dreaming is entering a new age—and he intends to shape it. Guided by visions and old oaths, he’s not here just to reclaim glory, but to build something transcendent from the ruins of belief.❧

Plot Hooks.

Revival. Albrecthine's return from Arcadia has created ripples through the Dreaming. Rumors swirl of ancient secrets stitched into his memory—places long forgotten, bargains made by lost monarchs, and whispers of a coming war among the Courts. He draws the attention of rival nobles and Banal institutions alike.

Signature Motif.

Violets and Cello. Albrecthine is always heralded by a faint scent of crushed violets and the haunting hum of a distant cello—notes that seem to bend light around him. His eyes shimmer with gold flecks that briefly reflect Arcadian landscapes, and he wears gloves spun of silver thread that glow faintly when truth is spoken nearby.

Perfect for...

Court Drama Albrecthine fits into mythic fantasy, court intrigue, and existential drama. Plots around him may explore lost oaths, fractured identity, and the seductive danger of Arcadian memory. Themes of beauty versus purpose, memory versus illusion, and the burden of nobility are ripe for exploration through his enigmatic journey.
Goal

Humanity.

Though he wears Alder Beauregard’s skin with dignity, Albrecthine knows the soul beneath is fading. To remain grounded, he must cultivate mortal relationships, artistic expression, and vulnerability—without becoming lost in them.
obstacle

Banality.

The more he embraces his Sidhe power and Arcadian nobility, the more his host's memories wither. Each act of glamour-summoning accelerates the process, threatening to turn him into a hollow vessel ruled by fae pride alone.
