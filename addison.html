Addison Blake
Addison Blake
The Mentor
Addison Blake is a formidable legal mind dedicated to championing the vulnerable. As a seasoned lawyer, Addison uses his legal acumen to defend those in need, ensuring justice prevails. Yet, beyond his professional facade, he's a man who loves the energy and enthusiasm of youth, finding companionship and inspiration in younger men who complement his wisdom with their vitality. In the courtroom and in his personal life, Addison is a force to be reckoned with, a defender of justice and a seeker of passion.
His journey through life has been a balancing act of legal prowess and personal desires. With a heart that champions the underprivileged, he's the embodiment of a modern knight, fighting for justice in the courtroom and love in his personal life. His legacy, a tapestry of law and desire, defines the enigmatic Mentor of the Chantry. ❧

Plot Hooks.

Legal support, social scenes and teaching. Any plots that have to do with defending the weak from the authorities and supporting those who can't support themselves in the legal terrain got his attention. Smut wise, that has to do with power, control and public display will be for him.

Signature Motif.

Smoking. He will constantly be seen with a cigarette in hand. This is either because it's one of the tools he uses to create magical effects or because he enjoys the taste of tobacco. Being an immortal helps a lot with not developing lung diseases.

Perfect for...

Power play and Teaching. Addison likes to take control, to take the lead and take over. He's a leader and likes to keep it that way on the bed. Also, if you are an inexperienced innocent young man then you probably want to meet him for him to teach you the ways of pleasure.
Goal

Reformation.

Having lived for quite a while, Addison understands that the downfall of the Order of Hermes is all due the inner corruption and intrigue that erodes from the bottom. He witnessed the last days of Doissetep and having survived such ordeals inspire him to train a new generation of collaborative and insightful Mages.
obstacle

Tradition.

Having experienced the intrigues and betrayal of power-hungry Cabals in the past, he understands that Rebellion must come to sweep away the old laws and set a new Codex that can lead and shape the Mages of current times. This is his reason to start training new talents. But old structures are boulders to push.
«Facta Non Praesumutur, Sed Probantur»

Paradigm

Why?
Divine Order, Earthly Chaos To Addison, the Divine is real. There are forces above him that work in mysterious ways. Mages, like him, have achieved a great understanding of said mysterious ways, which they call the Laws. As a Lawyer, his rule is to use said Laws in order to bring forth change. However, not everything is known, thus Ascension can only be grasped when you have achieved full Knowledge.

Practices

How?
Psionics, High Ritual Magick, Invigoration, Witchcraft: Reality is bent when by his hands when he uses laws upon laws. He's trained extensively to understand his mind and how it rules over his body, grasping control of his body to manifest his Will.

Backgrounds

Signature Assets

Eternal Youth
What started as a prize by a fellow Mentor and a friend, soon became a curse. His metabolism makes him slow to age, allowing him to experience history on the making.
Judge's Wisdom
Immune to supernatural feats that deal with emotional manipulation. His own inner emotional life is tempered into a stoic and decisive character. Though difficult, he is not immune to possession or mental control.
Enchanting Feature: Smile
A smile that charms, disarms and sometimes, disrobes. Can add a D6 to the dice pool when required.

Other Assets

L'Academie Concordia
One of the strongest and oldest Hermetic Chantries in all Canada. Recently rebuilt. By day, it's just another classical building in the city, turned into a specialized social studies library, but by night the mages confer the secrets of the Universe.
Ward
A character that is constantly in Addison's life and who requires his care. At times could be his grand nephew, Ander or a grand niece. Sometimes it could be a friend or even a Soul Flower. Writers are encouraged to take up this role if required.
Ring of Audacity
A magical ring given to him the day of his graduation as a lawyer and later enchanted when he became a Master of Ars Mentis. The ring grants it's bearer a small boost of confidence and authority, specially in matters against unjust authorities.
