Alden Leroy
The Lion
Alden Leroy was born into a life of privilege, blessed with loving parents, a stable home, and all imaginable luxuries. However, the tranquility shattered when a fateful day brought unwelcome visitors to his doorstep. It was revealed that his father had failed to fulfill his duties to the Technocracy, an organization relentless in eradicating the belief in real magic, replacing it with the power of science. Tragedy struck, leaving Alden's father dead at the hands of the Technocracy's merciless agents.
In the wake of this devastating loss, a primal fury consumed Alden, transforming him into a werelion for the first time. Driven by vengeance, he fixated on the Technocracy as the enemy, determined to avenge his father's demise by striking back at those who orchestrated it. Despite his initial revulsion towards magic, he found himself forming a curious alliance with a group of mages in his quest for retribution. United by a shared enemy, Alden's path now intertwined with the enigmatic world of magic and power, forever altering the course of his life as he walked the line between savagery and purpose.❧

Ways

Regeneration
Unless it's by fire, silver or by any other damage marked as aggravated, damage is reduced by 1 category before noted. Fera in any supernatural form can reduce damage 1 category in damage per round.
Tribe Spirit: Lion
Can add a D6 to any roll of Etiquette, Athletism and Intimidation when in the hunt of a prey.
Frenzy
Fera enter Crinos shape and ignore any impairments. They also reduce the effect dice two categories for mental abilities or effects against them. They cannot use gifts and usually they leave Frenzy once all of their enemies are dead or incapacitated, then they loose the Wolf.

Silver
Silver is specially malignant to Fera in supernatural form who will receive aggravated damage from it, upping one category in the silver damage.
Tribe Bane: Yava
Receive a D6 disadvantage if your voice is recorded, your mane is cut against your will or companions are harm in your presence and not revenged.
Rage
