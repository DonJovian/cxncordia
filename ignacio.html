Ignacio Cienfuegos
The Demon Lord
Ignacio Cienfuegos once stood as the charismatic manager of the Mexico City branch of the opulent Seventh Heaven Hotels, a man of faith whose public devotion to Catholic values masked a tangled inner life of secret desires and private shame. Beneath polished suits and rosary prayers, he harbored a hunger for control and intimacy that society—and his own doctrine—condemned. When depression swallowed him whole, he sought escape in death, leaving a husk ripe for possession.
Into that body stepped Alderoth, a radiant Namaru who once shone in the high courts of Heaven before the Fall. A rebel driven by divine frustration, Alderoth had once challenged God's silence, aching for the Creator’s hand to guide and nurture their shared creation. Now wearing Ignacio's flesh and name, Alderoth walks the polished halls of Montreal’s Seventh Heaven with a serene smile and storm-heavy purpose—haunted by guilt, yet determined to shepherd humanity through the oncoming reckoning.❧

Plot Hooks.

Damnation. Ignacio walks a fine line between redemption and damnation. Plot hooks may include uncovering the mystery behind his death, former lovers blackmailing him, or rival demons testing his loyalties. His hotel could serve as neutral ground—until Heaven or Hell demands its allegiance in the celestial war.

Signature Motif.

Attire Ignacio is always seen wearing an immaculate crimson handkerchief tucked into his suit pocket—a stark contrast against his otherwise conservative attire. This blood-red cloth, a silent symbol of his past sins and celestial fire, often moves unnaturally in still air, unsettling mortals and hinting at the divine fury caged within.

Perfect for...

Forbidden Desire He thrives in gothic noir and psychological horror settings, where identity, guilt, and faith clash violently. Plotlines of Forbidden desire, moral ambiguity, and power play are welcome. Themes of self-loathing, divine abandonment, and corrupted redemption are ideal.
Goal

Haven.

Ignacio aims to transform the Montreal branch of Seventh Heaven into a haven for Earthbound demons—a neutral sanctuary where the Fallen can meet, conspire, or rest without fear. To do so, he must carefully weave infernal influence into every facet of the hotel while maintaining its façade of luxury and peace.
obstacle

Secrecy.

The hotel’s board suspects Ignacio’s sudden changes—private renovations, increased security, and strange new staff hires. Worse, a local exorcist has picked up on spiritual tremors emanating from the property. With both mortal and celestial eyes watching, one misstep could unravel everything and expose Alderoth’s divine rebellion to friend and foe alike.

The Fallen

Faith

Manifestation
All demons are unable to grow old or suffer diseases. All damage (except aggravated) is down 1 category and it can be healed by Faith (except aggravated). They are immune to possession, mind control or illusion.
Faithful Visage
Quingu: Receives Wings to fly and has a D6 advantage if required. Effect dice in Ranged attacks and Subterfuge rolls against them are lowered one category and Effect dice in Charisma rolls are upped one category.
Advantage
Hitches in Subterfuge, Persuasion, Intimidation and Leadership rolls can be rerolled so long the action is related to obtain Faith or to gain a new follower.

Torment

Weaknesses
Most other demons have powers to harm Demons, Evocations and also True Faith can be very powerful against them.
Tormented Visage
When given into the Torment, the character receives a D6 advantage of Horns and Corrosive Spit that are added to the main result. Receives a powerful booming voice, a D6 advantage that is also added to the main result. His image cannot be seen in a mirror or camera.
Weakness
They cannot rerol Hitches in Subterfuge, Persuasion, Intimidation and Leadership rolls against other Demons.

Light and Faith
Lamp of Faith Detect mortals and their stored faith. Effect dice category determines the length of the power in Kilometers. It can detect them even in hiding. If required, it can be a D6 advantage on detection.
